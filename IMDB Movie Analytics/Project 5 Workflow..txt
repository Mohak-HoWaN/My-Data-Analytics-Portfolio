### **Column Usage for Each Task**

#### **A. Movie Genre Analysis**
- **Relevant Columns:**
  - `genres`: Analyze the distribution of genres.
  - `imdb_score`: Calculate descriptive statistics for each genre.
- **Approach:**
  - Split `genres` for movies with multiple genres.
  - Use `imdb_score` to compute statistics (mean, median, etc.).

---

#### **B. Movie Duration Analysis**
- **Relevant Columns:**
  - `duration`: Analyze movie length distribution.
  - `imdb_score`: Correlate duration with scores.
- **Approach:**
  - Compute descriptive statistics for `duration`.
  - Create a scatter plot of `duration` vs. `imdb_score`.

---

#### **C. Language Analysis**
- **Relevant Columns:**
  - `language`: Determine the most common languages.
  - `imdb_score`: Calculate statistics for each language.
- **Approach:**
  - Group movies by `language` and compute metrics using `imdb_score`.

---

#### **D. Director Analysis**
- **Relevant Columns:**
  - `director_name`: Group movies by director.
  - `imdb_score`: Compute average scores for each director.
- **Approach:**
  - Use `imdb_score` to rank directors based on average scores.
  - Highlight directors performing in the top percentile.

---

#### **E. Budget Analysis**
- **Relevant Columns:**
  - `budget`: Analyze financial investment.
  - `gross`: Calculate profit margins.
  - `imdb_score`: Evaluate budget impact on ratings.
- **Approach:**
  - Compute the correlation between `budget` and `gross`.
  - Identify movies with the highest `(gross - budget)`.
