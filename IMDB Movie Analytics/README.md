# IMDb Movie Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
The entertainment industry is highly data-driven. This project analyzes extensive IMDb datasets to identify critical trends driving movie success, audience ratings, and studio profitability. 

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the correlation between movie budgets, genres, and box office revenue.
- Identify the factors that contribute to high audience ratings on IMDb.
- Uncover historical trends in the film industry over the past decades.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Processing:** Microsoft Excel, Python (Pandas)
- **Visualization:** Advanced Excel Charts, Data Storytelling
- **Presentation:** Microsoft PowerPoint, Video Presentation

## 📊 Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning:** Addressed inconsistencies, missing values, and outliers in the IMDb dataset.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Explored distributions of ratings, budgets, and revenues across different genres.
3. **Trend Analysis:** Mapped out the evolution of movie runtimes, popular genres, and profitability over time.
4. **Executive Presentation:** Condensed complex data findings into a digestible PowerPoint deck and a video overview.

## 🚀 Results & Impact
- Discovered actionable insights regarding the most profitable movie genres.
- Identified the optimal budget ranges for maximizing return on investment (ROI).
- Delivered a strategic presentation summarizing findings for stakeholders in the film industry.
