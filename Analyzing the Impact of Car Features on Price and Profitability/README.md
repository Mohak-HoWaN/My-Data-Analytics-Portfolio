# Analytics on Impact of Car Features

## 📌 Project Overview
The automotive market is highly competitive, and pricing strategies are crucial for profitability. This project evaluates the impact of various vehicle specifications on the Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) and overall market profitability.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the relationship between car features (e.g., engine size, horsepower, dimensions) and the MSRP.
- Build predictive regression models to estimate car prices based on their features.
- Identify the most profitable vehicle segments in the market.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Analysis & Modeling:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn
- **Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Techniques:** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Multiple Linear Regression, Correlation Matrices

## 📊 Methodology
1. **Data Cleaning:** Processed the automotive dataset, handling categorical variables and missing numerical data.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Used correlation matrices and scatter plots to visualize the relationships between features and price.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Selected the most impactful features for the pricing model.
4. **Regression Modeling:** Trained and evaluated multiple regression models to predict the MSRP.

## 🚀 Results & Impact
- Successfully built a predictive model with high accuracy for estimating car prices.
- Identified that engine size and horsepower are the strongest predictors of MSRP.
- Segmented the market to highlight high-margin vehicle categories for manufacturers.
